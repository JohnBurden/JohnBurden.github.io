The **Solid Foundations** project investigates the capabilities of general-purpose AI models through systematic evaluation. We explore cognitively inspired AI evaluation methods, design experiments to analyze failure modes, and publish open-source tools to make assessments transparent.

This ongoing work aims to provide decision makers and researchers with reliable information about the strengths and limitations of emerging systems. By building stronger evaluation foundations, we hope to support the safe and beneficial development of advanced AI.
